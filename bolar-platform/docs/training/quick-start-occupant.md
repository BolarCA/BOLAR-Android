# Quick start: occupant portal (/occupant)

Demo account occupant@demo.bolar.test. For people who live in a unit under someone else's lease (a corporate lease or
a household member) and need to see their housing status, file requests and read shared documents.

## 1. Home (/occupant)

Screenshot (described): greeting "Welcome, <first name>"; a "My housing" card with the property and unit once your
lease is linked (an explanatory empty state before that); "My requests" with your open maintenance requests.

## 2. Requests (/occupant/requests)

Screenshot (described): the maintenance intake (heading "Maintenance") with the same "New request" form residents
use, or, when no lease is linked to your login, a notice "Your account cannot file requests" asking you to have the
property manager link your lease.

- Once linked, requests work exactly as in the resident guide: title, category, priority, description, entry
  permission, then a `WO-` number and status updates.

## 3. Documents (/occupant/documents)

Screenshot (described): a documents table with the files shared with you (house rules, move-in checklist) and their
classification.

## 4. Access

You see only your own housing. Other portals show the denied state.

Support: your property manager or corporate coordinator (tier 1), then
[../runbooks/support-escalation.md](../runbooks/support-escalation.md).
